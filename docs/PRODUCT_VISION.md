# Product Vision

PetFood Intelligence is a scan-first consumer app for dog and cat food.

The native iOS experience should help someone standing in a pet-food aisle understand a product in seconds:

- What is this food?
- Who makes it?
- Is it for dogs or cats?
- What form is it?
- What are the leading ingredients in label order?
- What nutrition facts are available?
- Which values are calculated dry-matter values?
- How confident are we in the data source?

The product must not create arbitrary health scores, winners, medical claims, or good/bad ingredient judgments.
